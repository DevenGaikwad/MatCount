# Project conduct

This academic collaboration welcomes respectful, evidence-based discussion. Treat teammates, faculty reviewers and outside contributors with courtesy; critique implementation and documentation rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, impersonation, fabricated evidence and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to a verified repository maintainer, not through a public issue containing personal details. Maintainers may ask for clarification, moderate discussions or restrict participation proportionately. This project-specific policy does not claim affiliation with or adoption of another organization's code of conduct.
